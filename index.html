The Copper Difference

What does "copper-lined" actually do?

In plain language: copper is naturally hostile to bacteria. When the inside of your pet's bowl is lined with real copper, germs that thrive in plastic scratches and steel scours simply can't multiply the same way. Slime films slow down. Water stays fresher between refills. Meal residue carries less risk.

It's the same reason hospitals use copper surfaces — now built into something your dog licks every single day. Pair that with an elevated stand for better mealtime posture, and you get cleaner feeding with less scrubbing for you.
