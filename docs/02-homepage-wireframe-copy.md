## SCROLL 2 — WHAT COPPER DOES (Discover/Educate)
- **H2:** What does "copper-lined" actually do?
- **Body:** In plain language: copper is naturally hostile to bacteria… slime films slow down. Water stays fresher between refills. Meal residue carries less risk. It's the same reason hospitals use copper surfaces — now built into something your dog licks every single day.
- **Stats:** 99% bacterial reduction* · 2x longer water freshness vs plastic · 60 sec weekly cleaning
- Footnote: laboratory-tested antimicrobial efficacy; see Materials & Safety.
