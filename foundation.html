Where It Goes

Transparency first.

  • Feeding programs: kibble and fresh food for partner shelters, prioritizing high-intake municipal pounds.
  • Bowl grants: copper-lined bowls donated to shelter feeding stations for hygiene.
  • TNR support: contributions to trap-neuter-return programs in partner communities.
  • Quarterly reports: published on this page and emailed to subscribers every quarter.
