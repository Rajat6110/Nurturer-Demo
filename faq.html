Sizing & Care

Match rim height to chest height: Small for puppies, kittens, and toy breeds; Medium for adult small-to-medium dogs; Large for big breeds and seniors. Cats do best with shallow dishes at any age. When torn between sizes, size up.
Mild soap + warm water weekly; wipe dry. Exterior coat tolerates low-heat dishwashing. Skip bleach, steel wool, and abrasive scouring pads. Engraved names stay crisp with normal care.
Pugs, bulldogs, and persians benefit most from elevation - raised rims reduce neck extension and slow gulping. Pair with our Slow-Feast spiral bowl for fast eaters.
