Why bacteria lose on copper.

When microbes land on a dry copper surface, copper ions punch holes in their cell membranes and disrupt their internal machinery - a process researchers call contact killing. Studies of copper touch surfaces in hospitals document dramatic reductions in bacterial load compared to stainless steel and plastic.
